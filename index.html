AI Preparedness in CS Education

A UWP research blog on AI preparedness in undergraduate computer science education.

Read the argument Explore questions
Featured argument

CS programs should teach AI preparedness instead of treating AI as a shortcut to police.

Undergraduate computer science students are already using AI systems to explain errors, draft code, test ideas, and study unfamiliar concepts. The central question is no longer whether these tools should exist in learning spaces. The better question is how students can learn to use them critically, ethically, and effectively.

Core claim

AI preparedness belongs in the curriculum because professional judgment now includes knowing when automation helps, when it harms, and how to verify the difference.
